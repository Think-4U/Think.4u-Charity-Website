Mr. Abdul Muqeeth

CEO – Chief Executive Officer
Brand Strategist Community Builder

Mr. Abdul Muqeeth is an entrepreneur, brand strategist, and community builder with proven experience across marketing, entrepreneurship, technology, business development, and community building.

Through building ventures, working with brands, leading student initiatives, and connecting with students across 30+ colleges and academic institutions, he has developed a strong understanding of how ideas, people, and opportunities can come together to create meaningful impact.

“His work focuses on education, youth empowerment, community development, and social impact, with a vision to build impactful initiatives and sustainable opportunities for communities.”

Smt. Mudrakola Padmaja

Director – Women Community Development
Distinguished Hindi Pandit Classical Dancer Cultural Ambassador

Smt. Mudrakola Padmaja is a distinguished Hindi Pandit, classical dancer, and dedicated social service contributor, known for her commitment to education, Indian cultural heritage, and community development.

As a Hindi Pandit, she has contributed to the promotion of the Hindi language and literature. A passionate classical dancer, she represents the richness of Indian traditional arts and culture. Through her dedication to classical dance, she strives to preserve and promote India's cultural heritage while inspiring younger generations to connect with traditional art forms.

Alongside her academic and cultural interests, Smt. Mudrakola Padmaja actively supports social service and community-oriented initiatives. Her involvement reflects a strong commitment to empowering women, nurturing educational opportunities, and building cultural awareness across communities.

“Dedicated to empowering women, preserving Indian cultural traditions, and creating vibrant community development programs that uplift individuals from every walk of life.”

Shri. Mudrakola Venkatesham

Director & Advisor
Retired Banker Former Member, Consumer Forum Public Service Veteran

Shri. Mudrakola Venkatesham is a retired banker with extensive professional experience in the banking and financial sector.

He has also served as a Member of the Consumer Forum, contributing significantly to consumer awareness, grievance redressal, public accountability, and the protection of consumer rights.

His rich professional experience, ethical governance, and lifelong public-service orientation reflect a strong commitment to integrity, social responsibility, and community welfare.

“Guiding Think.4U Trust with wisdom, financial prudence, ethical public administration, and compassionate counsel for sustainable community benefit.”

Mr. Enagandula Jagadish

Director – Youth Development
MNC Professional NSS Community Leader Grassroots Pioneer

Enagandula Jagadish is a professional working with a reputed multinational company (MNC), carrying a deep passion for service, community development, and creating positive social impact.

During his degree, Jagadish actively led NSS (National Service Scheme) activities, where he worked closely with rural communities and organized initiatives aimed at creating awareness, encouraging development, and bringing positive change through innovative ideas.

His community service journey included connecting directly with people and contributing to awareness and development initiatives in villages such as Chamanpally, Gangadhara, and Garepelli. These formative experiences instilled in him the vital importance of grassroots-level development and the power of bringing people together for a common purpose.

His Guiding Vision

“Serve with purpose, inspire through ideas, and create a positive impact in society.”

Mr. Kairamkonda Vignesh

Director – Innovation & Entrepreneurship
Tech Lead, E-Cell RoboCup Junior 2019 Startupedia’26

Vignesh Kairamkonda is an entrepreneur and technology enthusiast passionate about turning ideas into meaningful, real-world solutions. His interests span education, hardware, and emerging technologies, with a strong focus on creativity, technical problem-solving, and innovation.

His journey began through robotics and competitive innovation, including securing a third-place finish at RoboCup Junior South Zone in 2019, and continued through Startupedia’26 and multiple national-level hackathons.

As a Technical Lead at his college’s E-Cell, he actively works at the intersection of technology and entrepreneurship. His experiences have taught him that entrepreneurship is not just about having ideas, but about understanding problems, building, adapting, and creating real value.

“Championing youth tech incubation, hardware innovation, and practical problem-solving to address pressing societal challenges.”

Ms. Macha Vineela

Director – Legal Affairs
Advocate & Legal Professional LL.B. | MBA | B.Com M.Sc. Psychology

Ms. Macha Vineela is a qualified professional with an extraordinary multidisciplinary academic background. She holds a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Science in Psychology (M.Sc. Psychology), and Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.).

She is a practicing Legal Professional and Advocate, with extensive knowledge and expertise spanning corporate law, dispute resolution, business management, commerce, and human psychology.

Her rich qualifications enable her to bring a well-rounded, ethical perspective to professional governance, regulatory compliance, legal safeguards, and social-service initiatives for the Trust.

“Ensuring legal integrity, statutory compliance, and ethical governance across all operational facets of Think.4U Charitable Trust.”
