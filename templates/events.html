MoU • 9 Nov 2026

CMR Engineering College (Autonomous)

MoU for Academics, Research & Societal Activities

Think.4U Charitable Trust and CMR Engineering College (Autonomous), Hyderabad, signed a Memorandum of Understanding on 9th November 2026 to promote academic excellence and knowledge sharing, research and innovation, student development and skill enhancement, social responsibility and community engagement, and joint societal initiatives.

Academics Research Student Development Social Responsibility

With gratitude to the CMR leadership — Chief Patrons Sri Ch. Narasimha Reddy (Chairman), Sri Ch. Bhoopal Reddy (Vice-Chairman) and Sri Ch. Srisailam Reddy (Secretary & Correspondent); Patron Dr. M. Ashok (Principal); Coordinator Dr. A. Pramod Kumar (Dean of MoU & Skills).
