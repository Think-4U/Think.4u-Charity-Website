Ms. Madurakola Malavika

Founder & Chairperson

Ms. Madurakola Malavika is an accomplished academician, startup mentor, researcher, innovation strategist, and social entrepreneur, currently serving as Head Innovations & Entrepreneurship at Vignan Group, Hyderabad. She is dedicated to nurturing innovation, entrepreneurship, research, and industry–academia collaboration.

She is the Founder & Chairman of Think.4U Charitable Trust and Founder of Think.4U Technologies, working tirelessly towards empowering youth, women innovators, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs across the nation.

As a startup mentor, she has mentored 80+ startups, supported entrepreneurs through guidance and investment, and has 159+ mentees actively working on startups at the national level, with an extensive professional network of 1,500+ founders.

She is an active Mentor on the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP), a flagship initiative by NITI Aayog, and a mentor at SAYUJ – The Startup Community Network. She is also closely associated with TiE Hyderabad and various national and international academic and research organizations.

A prolific researcher, she has contributed to 20+ Scopus-indexed journals and international peer-reviewed publications, along with books, book chapters, and editorial reviews. She has served as an Advisory Board Member for international journals and has contributed to innovation through patents in AI-enabled work culture and employee performance measurement.

Awards & Recognitions

Founding Vision

“Her vision is to build sustainable innovation ecosystems that transform education, research, and ideas into impactful entrepreneurial ventures for nation-building.”

Mr. B Karthik Reddy

Trustee & Treasurer

Sri B. Karthik Reddy is an accomplished educationist, social activist, and community leader who holds a Master’s degree in Business Administration (MBA).

He serves as the Correspondent and Secretary of Teja Vidyalayam, where he is passionately committed to strengthening quality education and creating opportunities for students to develop their academic, professional, and personal potential.

With a strong passion for education, social service, and community development, he actively works towards empowering students and supporting grassroots initiatives that contribute to the betterment of society.

His leadership reflects a steadfast commitment to promoting accessible education, youth development, and social responsibility. As Treasurer of Think.4U Charitable Trust, he oversees fiduciary integrity, financial planning, and responsible resource deployment.

“Through his work in the education sector and social initiatives, Sri B. Karthik Reddy continues to contribute meaningfully to the development of young minds and the community.”

Mr. K Vijay Krishna

Trustee – Community Development

Mr. K Vijay Krishna is a dedicated professional and social-service enthusiast committed to creating a positive impact in society. He believes firmly in the values of compassion, integrity, responsibility, and inclusive development.

As a management professional with a specialization in Finance, he brings a structured, analytical, and responsible approach to charitable and social initiatives. He is actively associated with community-oriented programs aimed at supporting marginalized groups and advancing societal welfare.

His vision is to encourage education, empowerment, and opportunities for individuals from diverse socio-economic backgrounds. Through his initiatives, he strives to promote kindness, dignity, and sustainable community development.

“He strongly believes that meaningful change begins with responsible individuals and collective action. With a spirit of service and leadership, he continues to work toward building a more supportive and empowered society.”

Sai Harishith Somishetti

Trustee

Entrepreneur | Founder & Chairman | Cybersecurity Enthusiast | Youth Leader | Member of the Indian Youth Parliament

Sai Harishith Somishetti is a young Indian entrepreneur, technology innovator, cybersecurity enthusiast, and youth leader driven by a vision to create meaningful impact through entrepreneurship, technology, innovation, and social responsibility.

Beginning his entrepreneurial journey at just 15 years of age, Sai Harishith has continuously worked towards building organizations, developing innovative solutions, empowering aspiring entrepreneurs, and creating opportunities for the next generation. He believes that age should never be a limitation to leadership, innovation, or the pursuit of ambitious ideas.

Entrepreneurial Leadership

He is the Founder & Chairman of SHIELDNET GROUPS, a multi-venture organization operating across technology, cybersecurity, creative industries, media production, and public relations. Under SHIELDNET GROUPS, he leads and manages multiple ventures including:

  • SHIELDNET SOLUTIONS – Software development, IT services, digital transformation, and cybersecurity.
  • SHIELDNET CREATIVES – Creative advertising, branding, digital marketing, and brand development.
  • TECHNORIX GLOBAL IT SOLUTIONS – Technology-driven products and innovative IT solutions.
  • GLOBAL PR 360 – Public relations, strategic communication, and brand positioning.
  • BIG DADDY PRODUCTION HOUSE – Film production, short films, and creative media.
  • VCN – Vigilant Cyber Ninjas – An ethical hacking and cybersecurity community focused on digital safety, cybersecurity awareness, and skill development.

Through these initiatives, he aims to establish an ecosystem that connects technology, entrepreneurship, creativity, and social impact.

National Youth Leadership & Public Engagement

As a Member of the Indian Youth Parliament, Sai Harishith engages with youth-oriented initiatives and opportunities for civic participation, leadership, and national development. He strongly believes that young people should actively participate in nation-building, contribute innovative ideas, understand public issues, and become responsible leaders of tomorrow. His involvement in youth leadership reflects his commitment to encouraging young minds to move beyond conventional boundaries and actively contribute to society.

Cybersecurity & Social Impact

As the Founder of VCN – Vigilant Cyber Ninjas, he has been working towards developing a cybersecurity ecosystem that encourages ethical hacking, responsible security research, digital awareness, and women's digital safety. Under his leadership, VCN has grown into a community of 4,500+ ethical hackers.

  • Promoting ethical hacking and cybersecurity education.
  • Creating awareness about cybercrime and online safety.
  • Encouraging responsible vulnerability research.
  • Supporting women's digital safety initiatives.
  • Developing practical cybersecurity skills among students.
  • Building a network of cybersecurity enthusiasts and young innovators.

  • Contributed to student development, digital awareness, and social-impact initiatives.

Technology & Innovation

With a strong interest in emerging technologies, he actively explores cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), software development, digital infrastructure, and technology-driven entrepreneurship. He believes in transforming ideas into practical solutions and encouraging students to gain real-world experience through projects, innovation challenges, workshops, and startup initiatives. His approach combines technical knowledge, entrepreneurial thinking, organizational leadership, and a strong commitment to continuous learning.

Vision & Mission

His long-term vision is to build globally impactful organizations, create opportunities for aspiring entrepreneurs, strengthen India's technology ecosystem, and inspire young people to become innovators, responsible leaders, and nation-builders. He aims to contribute towards a future where technology serves humanity, entrepreneurship creates opportunities, and youth leadership drives meaningful change.

Philosophy
