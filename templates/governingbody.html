Ms. Madurakola Malavika

Founder & Chairperson
Head Innovations @ Vignan WEP NITI Aayog Mentor TiE Hyderabad

Ms. Madurakola Malavika is an accomplished academician, startup mentor, researcher, innovation strategist, and social entrepreneur, currently serving as Head Innovations & Entrepreneurship at Vignan Group, Hyderabad. She is dedicated to nurturing innovation, entrepreneurship, research, and industry–academia collaboration.

She is the Founder & Chairman of Think.4U Charitable Trust and Founder of Think.4U Technologies, working tirelessly towards empowering youth, women innovators, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs across the nation.

As a startup mentor, she has mentored 80+ startups, supported entrepreneurs through guidance and investment, and has 159+ mentees actively working on startups at the national level, with an extensive professional network of 1,500+ founders.

She is an active Mentor on the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP), a flagship initiative by NITI Aayog, and a mentor at SAYUJ – The Startup Community Network. She is also closely associated with TiE Hyderabad and various national and international academic and research organizations.

A prolific researcher, she has contributed to 20+ Scopus-indexed journals and international peer-reviewed publications, along with books, book chapters, and editorial reviews. She has served as an Advisory Board Member for international journals and has contributed to innovation through patents in AI-enabled work culture and employee performance measurement.

Mr. B Karthik Reddy

Trustee & Treasurer
MBA Graduate Secretary, Teja Vidyalayam Community Leader

Sri B. Karthik Reddy is an accomplished educationist, social activist, and community leader who holds a Master’s degree in Business Administration (MBA).

He serves as the Correspondent and Secretary of Teja Vidyalayam, where he is passionately committed to strengthening quality education and creating opportunities for students to develop their academic, professional, and personal potential.

With a strong passion for education, social service, and community development, he actively works towards empowering students and supporting grassroots initiatives that contribute to the betterment of society.

His leadership reflects a steadfast commitment to promoting accessible education, youth development, and social responsibility. As Treasurer of Think.4U Charitable Trust, he oversees fiduciary integrity, financial planning, and responsible resource deployment.

“Through his work in the education sector and social initiatives, Sri B. Karthik Reddy continues to contribute meaningfully to the development of young minds and the community.”

Mr. K Vijay Krishna

Trustee – Community Development
Management in Finance Social Service Enthusiast Inclusive Development

Mr. K Vijay Krishna is a dedicated professional and social-service enthusiast committed to creating a positive impact in society. He believes firmly in the values of compassion, integrity, responsibility, and inclusive development.

As a management professional with a specialization in Finance, he brings a structured, analytical, and responsible approach to charitable and social initiatives. He is actively associated with community-oriented programs aimed at supporting marginalized groups and advancing societal welfare.

His vision is to encourage education, empowerment, and opportunities for individuals from diverse socio-economic backgrounds. Through his initiatives, he strives to promote kindness, dignity, and sustainable community development.

“He strongly believes that meaningful change begins with responsible individuals and collective action. With a spirit of service and leadership, he continues to work toward building a more supportive and empowered society.”

Mr. M. Himavanth Kumar

Trustee
6+ Years Industry Experience Tata Motors & Citroën Alum Tata AIA Life Insurance

Mr. M. Himavanth Kumar is a dedicated and versatile professional with 6+ years of rich industry experience in the automobile and financial services sector, having served in leadership and sales-management roles at leading automotive brands including Tata Motors and Citroën.

He is currently associated with Tata AIA Life Insurance Company in an office-support role, bringing valuable cross-functional acumen to organizational logistics and administration.

Experienced in team leadership, sales coordination, customer relationship management, operational support, and administration, he possesses a strong ability to adapt to diverse professional environments and steer community mobilization for the Trust.

“Applying professional operational rigor, team leadership, and administrative excellence to amplify grassroots impact across all Think.4U Trust initiatives.”
