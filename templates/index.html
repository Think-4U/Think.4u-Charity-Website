About Think.4U Charitable Trust

“Think. Empower. Uplift.”

A public charitable trust established to empower communities through quality education, skill development, preventive healthcare, environmental sustainability, and holistic social welfare initiatives.

Think.4U Foundation

Indian Trusts Act, 1882

Meaningful change begins with thoughtful action. We partner with volunteers, academicians, and corporate CSR wings to bring lasting, scalable impact.

Education & Youth Scholarships

Health, Nutrition & Hygiene

Organizing health camps, blood donation drives, mental wellbeing sessions, and menstrual hygiene drives.

Women Empowerment & Skill Building

Vocational training, handicrafts, tailoring, and micro-entrepreneurship support for self-reliance.

Environmental Sustainability & Relief

Tree plantation drives (2,000+ saplings), clean-water initiatives, and disaster relief programs.
