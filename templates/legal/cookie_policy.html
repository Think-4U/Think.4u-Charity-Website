Cookie Policy

Information regarding how Think4U Charity Organization uses cookies and local storage technologies on https://think4u.org.

Privacy-First Commitment

We only use strictly essential cookies for secure logins, CSRF defense, and user preferences. We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or commercial tracking pixels.

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or unique identifiers placed on your browser or device when visiting a website. They enable websites to recall your session, maintain secure logins, and remember display choices across pages.

2 How Think4U Uses Cookies

Think4U uses cookies and browser local storage strictly for the following functional purposes:

Session & Authentication:

Maintaining active user sessions for registered donors, volunteers, coordinators, and administrators.

Security & CSRF Defense:

Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) security tokens that verify that requests originate from legitimate users.

Consent Preferences:

Remembering that you have acknowledged our policies so the consent modal does not repeatedly interrupt your visits.

Basic UI Preferences:

Transient interface state, such as dismissed banners and navigation adjustments.

3 Analytics Tools Status

Think4U currently does NOT use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, or similar third-party analytics/advertising tracking systems.

If modern privacy-respecting analytics are ever introduced in the future to improve website usability, this Policy will be updated and users will be notified with appropriate opt-in controls.

4 Third-Party Services

Integrated third-party services—such as Cashfree for payment processing or Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection—may independently set security cookies to prevent fraud and execute secure transactions under their respective privacy policies.

5 Managing & Disabling Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies through browser settings. You can also block cookies entirely. However, please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent secure login, donor account access, or receipt downloads from functioning properly.

Think4U may update this Cookie Policy when technology, statutory requirements, or Website functionality changes. The updated version will display the revised Effective Date.
