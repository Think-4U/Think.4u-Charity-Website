India-Only Donation & FCRA Notice

Statutory notice regarding domestic donations and Foreign Contribution Regulation Act (FCRA) compliance.

Domestic Donations Only

Think4U Charity Organization currently accepts donations exclusively from within India and from Indian bank accounts / domestic payment methods through its Website.

• Technical Location Controls: The Website uses technical location controls and IP-based geolocation checks to restrict access from outside India.

• International Contributions Prohibited: International or foreign contributions are currently NOT accepted through think4u.org.

• FCRA Application Pending: Think4U has formally applied for applicable Foreign Contribution Regulation Act (FCRA) authorisation with the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. Until required statutory authorisation is granted and effective, Think4U does not solicit or accept foreign contributions through this Website.

• No Circumvention: Users outside India must not attempt to bypass geographical or payment restrictions through VPNs, proxies, or foreign card payment gateways to donate.

• Future Operations: Foreign contribution arrangements, if legally permitted in the future, will be officially communicated through an updated policy and separate international donation channel.

Why does this restriction exist?

Under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (FCRA) in India, charitable non-governmental organizations must hold a valid FCRA registration or prior permission from the Central Government before receiving any donation or contribution from a foreign source (including foreign citizens, foreign corporations, or international cards).

Think4U strictly abides by Indian statutory norms and has temporarily restricted online donations to domestic sources within India.
