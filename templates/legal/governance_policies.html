Legal, Compliance, Governance & Risk Management Policies

Comprehensive organisation-level governance and risk management framework for Think4U Charity Organization.

Organisational Policy Framework:

This section establishes the organisation-level policy framework for Think4U Charity Organization. These policies complement the Website Terms, Privacy Policy, Donation & Refund Policy, Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy, and other operational procedures. They are aligned with the Trust Deed, statutory registrations, approvals, and actual charitable operations.

14.1 Legal Policy

Purpose: Think4U will conduct its charitable activities in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, its Trust Deed, and applicable directions of competent authorities.

Core Legal Principles:

• Maintain accurate legal and statutory registration records.

• Operate only within the charitable objects and powers applicable to the Trust.

• Maintain required statutory, financial, donation, and organisational records.

• Use contracts and written approvals where appropriate for material third-party relationships.

• Protect donor, volunteer, beneficiary, employee, and user rights.

• Respect intellectual property, privacy, payment, cybersecurity, and consumer protection requirements.

• Seek professional legal advice for material disputes, regulatory notices, contracts, and Trust status changes.

Legal Requests: Think4U cooperates with lawful requests from courts, regulators, government authorities, and law-enforcement agencies. Information will be disclosed only as permitted or required by applicable law.

14.2 Compliance Policy

Purpose: Maintain a compliance framework intended to identify, prevent, document, and address legal, regulatory, financial, operational, and ethical compliance obligations.

• Trust registration & charitable trust law
• Income tax & donation accounting
• 80G status & tax representations
• 12A/12AB charitable registrations
• FCRA foreign contribution restrictions
• Cashfree payment gateway norms
• Data protection & digital privacy
• Cybersecurity & access controls
No Misrepresentation: Think4U must not represent that it holds a registration, approval, tax benefit, FCRA authorisation, certification or licence that it does not currently hold. Website claims must strictly reflect actual status.

14.3 Governance Policy

Purpose: Maintain governance practices intended to support accountability, transparency, responsible decision-making, and protection of charitable resources.

Governance Principles: Decisions are made in accordance with the Trust Deed and applicable law. Material financial decisions require appropriate authorization. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed and managed. Charitable funds must be used exclusively for legitimate organisational purposes.

Appointment Order Mandate & Non-Enrichment Covenant: As explicitly stipulated in every official appointment order, resolution, and agreement issued by Think.4U Charitable Trust, no trustee, director, executive, employee, or volunteer shall generate, solicit, accept, or divert funds in the name of the Trust for personal benefit or private gain. All fundraising, donor engagements, and institutional contributions must strictly flow through official Trust banking accounts and authorized gateway interfaces.

Financial & Beneficiary Governance: Donation funds are reconciled regularly with Cashfree and banking records. Programmes involving children or vulnerable persons incorporate strict safeguarding and consent protocols.

14.4 Risk Management Policy

Purpose: Identify, assess, manage, monitor, and review risks that could affect beneficiaries, donors, volunteers, finances, IT systems, reputation, legal compliance, or charitable mission.

Risk Categories: Legal & regulatory, financial & fraud, donation/payment gateway, cybersecurity, privacy, operational, safeguarding, third-party vendor, business continuity, and FCRA compliance.

Risk Treatment: Avoid, Reduce (procedural & technical controls), Transfer (contracts/insurance), or Accept (documented residual risk).

14.5 Information Security & Access Control Policy

  • Administrative access is role-based on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Privileged accounts require strong authentication and multi-factor verification where supported.
  • Credentials and passwords must never be shared or committed to repositories.
  • Production data is not copied to development environments without anonymization.
  • Security vulnerabilities are managed under our Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy.
  • Payment credentials are processed exclusively by Cashfree and never stored locally.

14.6 Incident Response Policy

Think4U maintains a documented incident response procedure: Identify → Contain → Preserve Evidence → Assess Impact → Notify Management & Legal Advisers → Notify Authorities within statutory timelines → Coordinate with Cashfree/hosting partners → Remediate root cause → Document lessons learned.

14.7 Third-Party & Vendor Risk Policy

Material third-party services (Cashfree, Google Authentication, Supabase, Cloudflare, IP-API) are assessed for security and privacy practices prior to integration. Access granted to third parties is strictly limited to necessary functions.

14.8 Anti-Fraud & Financial Integrity Policy

Zero Tolerance for Personal Enrichment: No person associated with Think.4U Charitable Trust may generate money, receive gifts, or solicit donations in the name of the Trust for personal benefit. As specified clearly in all official appointment orders, any unauthorized fundraising, diversion of contributions to personal bank accounts/wallets, or commercial use of the Trust's goodwill is strictly forbidden.

Fraud, theft, embezzlement, manipulation of donation records, and unauthorized personal use of organisational funds are strictly prohibited. Donation records are reconciled against Cashfree transaction records. Staff and volunteers must never divert donations. Violations will result in immediate termination of appointment and initiation of civil and criminal legal action under Indian law.

14.9 Anti-Bribery & Ethical Conduct Policy

Think4U prohibits bribery, kickbacks, and improper payments in connection with any charitable or administrative activity. Personnel and representatives must not offer or accept improper benefits to influence decisions.

14.10 Safeguarding Policy

Beneficiaries must be treated with dignity and respect. No exploitation, abuse, harassment, or discrimination is tolerated. Appropriate consent is obtained for photographs/videos. Identifying information of vulnerable children is safeguarded. Volunteers working with beneficiaries undergo screening and supervision.

14.11 Records Management & Retention Policy

Accurate records necessary for legal, tax, accounting, donation, governance, and volunteer operations are maintained securely against unauthorized alteration. Records are retained in accordance with applicable statutory periods under Indian law.

14.12 Whistleblowing / Confidential Reporting Policy

Think4U provides a confidential channel for personnel, volunteers, and stakeholders to raise genuine concerns about fraud, corruption, serious legal violations, safeguarding lapses, or misuse of funds. Retaliation against good-faith whistleblowers is strictly prohibited. Reports can be submitted to ceo@think4u.org or info@think4u.org.

14.13 Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Policy

Critical services—including website access, donation processing, user authentication, and donation receipts—have automated backups and continuity plans to restore operations promptly in the event of unexpected outages.

14.14 Policy Review & Approval

These policies are reviewed at least annually and whenever there is a material change in law, regulatory status, Trust structure, donation/payment system, or organisational operations.

Approval Authority: Board of Trustees / Authorised Management

Review Frequency: At least annually
