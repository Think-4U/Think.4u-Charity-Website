Types of Matters Handled

You may register a grievance concerning any of the following matters:

Donations & Payments:

Duplicate payments, payment deductions without receipt, transaction status confirmation, or refund inquiries.

User Accounts & Privacy:

Login issues, account deletion, profile updates, data privacy rights, or unauthorized activity reports.

Volunteer & Coordination:

Volunteer onboarding status, certificate issuance, program scheduling, or event feedback.

Safeguarding & Content:

Beneficiary privacy concerns, photograph/video takedown requests, ethics, or general conduct.

Information to Include in Your Grievance

To expedite resolution, please provide:

  • Your Full Name and Registered Email / Mobile Number.
  • Transaction / Reference ID (for donation or payment-related matters).
  • Date of occurrence and clear, factual description of the issue.
  • Any supporting documents or screenshots.
Security Warning: Do NOT send passwords, complete card numbers, CVVs, or OTPs in any email or communication.

Investigation & Resolution Workflow

1
Acknowledgment: Grievances are acknowledged within 24 to 48 business hours with an acknowledgment reference.
2
Verification: Think4U may request additional records reasonably necessary to verify identity or transaction logs with Cashfree/banks.
3
Resolution: Think4U strives to resolve all grievances expeditiously, ordinarily within 7 to 15 working days. Privacy grievances are handled strictly in compliance with applicable data protection norms.
