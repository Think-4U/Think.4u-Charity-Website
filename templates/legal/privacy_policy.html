Privacy Policy

Think4U Charity Organization (“Think4U”, “we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information processed through https://think4u.org.

Think4U Charity Organization

2 Information We Collect

Identity & Contact:

Name, email address, mobile/telephone number, postal address, and date of birth where provided or required for receipt issuance or volunteer onboarding.

Donation Data:

Donation amount, selected project/cause, donation date/time, transaction/reference ID, payment status/method, receipt information, and reconciliation records.

Account & Auth:

Email, mobile number, account identifiers, authentication logs, login method, activity logs, and profile preferences.

Technical Information:

IP address, browser/device information, operating system, approximate IP-derived location, access times, pages visited, security/diagnostic events, and essential cookies.

Volunteer Data: Information submitted through our volunteer registration facilities, including skills, interests, and availability.

3 Why We Use Personal Information

To manage accounts, authenticate users, process and reconcile donations, confirm payments, issue receipts, provide charitable services, process volunteer registrations, respond to enquiries, provide user support, send transactional and organisational communications, maintain system security, prevent fraud, enforce our Terms of Service, comply with legal and statutory regulatory requirements, maintain accounting records, respond to lawful authority requests, and protect the rights, property, and safety of our beneficiaries and organisation.

4 Payment Processing

Payments are processed through Cashfree and applicable secure payment infrastructure.

Think4U does not intentionally store complete card numbers, CVV information, or complete payment credentials on its own servers.

Cashfree may process payment information under its own applicable terms and privacy documentation.

5 Geolocation and India-Only Access

Think4U uses IP-based geolocation to determine whether a connection originates from India.

The Website currently uses IP-API for this purpose.

The purpose is to enforce India-only access and prevent unauthorized access or international donation attempts from outside India.

Note: IP geolocation is not perfectly accurate and legitimate Indian users may occasionally experience restrictions. Users must not circumvent controls by misrepresenting their geographical location.

6 Authentication and Social Login

The Website may permit login using email, mobile number, and Google / social-login functionality.

When using a third-party authentication provider, that provider may process information under its own privacy policy.

Think4U generally receives only information necessary to authenticate and maintain your account.

6A Third-Party Authentication and Google Login

Think4U may allow account access using Google and other supported social-login providers.

If you choose third-party login, Think4U may receive information made available by the provider for authentication/account management, such as name, email address, profile identifier, and other information permitted by the configured authentication scope.

Think4U uses such information for account creation/maintenance, authentication, account association, Website functionality, and security.

Think4U does not request or receive your third-party account password through standard OAuth authentication.

The third-party provider’s own terms and privacy policy also apply.

Google Sign-In permissions will be strictly limited to configured scopes actually needed by the Website (basic profile & email). Additional permissions will not be requested unless necessary, appropriately disclosed, and configured.

Users may use another available login method (e.g. mobile OTP or direct email) where provided.

If third-party access is revoked, certain functions may become unavailable until another authentication method is associated.

Think4U may retain previously received account information where reasonably necessary for account management, security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or other legitimate purposes, subject to law.

7 Cookies

Cookies may be used for login sessions, authentication, CSRF security, fraud prevention, user preferences, and essential Website functions.

Think4U currently does NOT use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, or similar analytics/advertising tracking tools. Any changes will be updated in our Cookie Policy.

8 Communications

Communications may include donation/payment confirmations, receipts, account/security notices, charity/project updates, campaigns, and newsletters where applicable. Users may contact Think4U regarding communications they no longer wish to receive. Transactional, security, and statutory legal communications may still be sent.

9 Sharing of Information

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to Cashfree/payment providers, banks/financial institutions, authentication providers, hosting/infrastructure providers, email/SMS providers, security/fraud providers, geolocation providers, professional advisers, auditors/accountants, regulators, courts, government authorities, law enforcement, and service providers acting for Think4U. We do not sell personal data.

10 International / Third-Party Processing

Some third-party technical providers may process information using infrastructure outside India. Where applicable, such processing is subject to relevant contractual, technical, and legal safeguards.

11 Data Security

Think4U uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information. Measures include access controls, authentication, SSL/TLS encryption in transit, logging, security monitoring, restricted administration, and backup/recovery. However, no internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

12 Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for user accounts, donation reconciliation, accounting, statutory tax records, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and security auditing. When no longer required and no statutory reason exists to retain it, information is securely deleted or anonymised.

13 Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion where available, withdrawal of consent where applicable, and grievances. Requests may be sent to info@think4u.org. Think4U may verify identity before processing requests.

14 Children’s Information

The Website may contain photographs/videos involving children or beneficiaries as part of charitable activities. Think4U implements appropriate safeguarding and permissions. The Website is not intended to encourage children to independently donate or provide unnecessary personal information. Where parental/guardian consent is legally required, Think4U adheres to applicable requirements.

15 Beneficiary Photographs and Videos

Photographs/videos may be collected or published for charitable programmes, events, and beneficiary activities. Think4U seeks appropriate permissions and follows strict safeguarding/privacy guidelines. Privacy or safeguarding concerns may be reported directly to info@think4u.org.

16 Data Breaches

If Think4U becomes aware of a personal-data breach requiring notification under applicable law, it will promptly take appropriate mitigation, investigation, and legal/regulatory notification steps.

17 Third-Party Websites

Third-party links may appear on the Website. Think4U does not control and is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external websites.

18 Changes to Privacy Policy

Think4U may update this Policy when services, technology, legal obligations, or processing practices change. The revised version will show an updated date.
