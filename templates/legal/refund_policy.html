Donation, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Guidelines and procedures governing voluntary charitable contributions made to Think4U Charity Organization through https://think4u.org.

General Principle: Non-Refundable Contributions

Charitable donations are voluntary contributions intended to support social welfare and humanitarian projects. Once executed, donations are generally non-refundable except in demonstrated cases of technical duplication or banking errors.

1 General Policy

Think4U accepts one-time donations. Donations are voluntary contributions supporting charitable activities and selected causes/projects. Donors receive an official acknowledgment and electronic receipt for eligible contributions.

2 Payment Methods

Donations are processed through Cashfree Payments. Currently supported payment methods include UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), debit cards, credit cards, net banking across major Indian banks, and approved domestic digital wallets.

3 Cancellation

A completed donation generally cannot be cancelled after successful processing. If a transaction is pending or failed while the donor was debited by their bank, please contact Think4U promptly with the transaction details.

4 Refunds

Donations are generally non-refundable. Think4U may consider refunds exclusively in the following specific circumstances:

  • Duplicate payments debited due to browser refresh or network glitch.
  • Technical error where funds were debited but the transaction was not recorded in our system.
  • Payment-processing gateway errors or double charging.
  • Where a refund is mandated by applicable regulatory authorities or law.

Please note: Refunds are not ordinarily provided merely because a donor changes their mind after contributing.

5 Refund Request Procedure

Send refund requests to info@think4u.org with the following mandatory details:

• Full Name of Donor

• Registered Email Address and Mobile Number

• Cashfree Transaction / Order Reference ID

• Date and Exact Amount Debited

• Reason for Request with supporting bank debit statement / screenshot

6 Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally credited back using the original payment mechanism. Actual credit timelines depend on Cashfree, acquiring banks, card networks, or UPI payment infrastructure (typically 5 to 7 business days from approval).

7 Fraudulent Transactions

Report suspected unauthorised or fraudulent transactions immediately to Think4U and your issuing bank/card provider. Think4U will investigate and fully cooperate with banks and law enforcement authorities.

8 Chargebacks

Payment disputes and chargebacks will be resolved following applicable banking and payment-network procedures. Think4U provides accurate transaction logs and donor acknowledgment records to Cashfree and relevant financial institutions.

9 Policy Changes

Think4U may update this policy as payment arrangements, statutory banking laws, or operational procedures change.
