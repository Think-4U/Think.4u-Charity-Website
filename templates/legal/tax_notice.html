Important Notice for All Donors: Section 80G Status

Think4U Charity Organization’s application / approval relating to Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act is currently under process with the relevant government authority.

• No Immediate 80G Deduction Assumption: Donors should not currently assume that donations made through this Website qualify for an Income Tax deduction under Section 80G.

• Eligibility Criteria: Any future tax deduction or benefit is strictly subject to Think4U receiving its formal order of approval, the effective date thereof, donor eligibility, transaction records, and documentation requirements under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

• No Guarantee of Tax Benefit: Think4U does not warrant or guarantee that any particular donation will qualify for a tax deduction until formal government approval is effective.

• Updates: Once applicable statutory approval is granted and effective, this notice and all donor receipts will be formally updated.

• Independent Consultation: For specific questions regarding your tax treatment, donors are advised to consult their personal chartered accountant or tax advisor.

Frequently Asked Questions regarding 80G

What is Section 80G?

Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act allows donors to claim deductions on donations made to approved charitable organizations. This benefit requires formal government registration.

Will I still receive a donation receipt?

Yes. All donors receive an official digital receipt with a unique donation reference number, date, amount, and payment details confirming receipt of funds by Think4U Charitable Trust.

When will 80G status be updated?

Our application is filed and progressing through standard administrative verification. Once approval is issued by the Income Tax Department, the status on our portal, receipts, and footer will be updated immediately.
