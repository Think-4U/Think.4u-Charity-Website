Ms. Madurakola Malavika

Managing Trustee

Ms. Madurakola Malavika is an accomplished academician, startup mentor, researcher, innovation strategist, and social entrepreneur, currently serving as Head Innovations & Entrepreneurship at Vignan Group, Hyderabad. She is dedicated to nurturing innovation, entrepreneurship, research, and industry–academia collaboration.

She is the Founder & Chairman of Think.4U Charitable Trust and Founder of Think.4U Technologies, working towards empowering youth, women innovators, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs.

As a startup mentor, she has mentored 80+ startups, supported entrepreneurs through guidance and investment, and has 159+ mentees actively working on startups at the national level, with a professional network of 1,500+ founders.

She is a Mentor on the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP), a NITI Aayog initiative, and a mentor at SAYUJ – The Startup Community Network. She is also associated with TiE Hyderabad and various national and international academic and research organizations.

A prolific researcher, she has contributed to 20+ Scopus-indexed journals and international peer-reviewed publications, along with books, book chapters, editorial and peer-review activities. She has served as an Advisory Board Member for international journals and has contributed to innovation through patents in AI-enabled work culture and employee performance measurement.

Mr. Abdul Muqeeth

CEO – Chief Executive Officer

Mr. Abdul Muqeeth is an entrepreneur, brand strategist, and community builder with proven experience across marketing, entrepreneurship, technology, business development, and community building.

Through building ventures, collaborating with prominent brands, leading student initiatives, and connecting with students and student leaders across 30+ colleges and universities, he has developed a deep, practical understanding of how ideas, people, and opportunities can come together to create transformative social impact.

As Chief Executive Officer (CEO), his operational leadership guides program design, volunteer coordination, digital platforms, and cross-sectoral partnerships for the Foundation.

Core Focus & Vision

“His work focuses on education, youth empowerment, community development, and social impact, with a vision to build impactful initiatives and sustainable opportunities for communities.”
