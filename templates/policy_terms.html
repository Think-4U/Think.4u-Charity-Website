By using Think.4U, you agree to use the platform responsibly and not misuse donation, volunteer, or grievance workflows. All content, images, and data belong to Think.4U Charitable Trust. Unauthorized reproduction is prohibited.

We collect only information required to provide services such as donations, receipts, volunteer management, and communication. Personal data such as name, email, and phone are stored securely and never sold or shared with third parties without consent.
